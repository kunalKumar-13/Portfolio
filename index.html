01 / Memory · local-first

Recall

“Never lose the thread.”

Work scatters — the tabs, the files, the half-finished chat. Recall reconstructs what you were working on and hands it back the moment you return. One keystroke, 100% on your machine: no cloud, no telemetry, plain files you can read and delete. A browser extension and watchers stream signals into a local daemon that scores recovery and ranks what mattered; a Qt launcher puts you back mid-thought.

02 / Trust & safety

Aegis New

Moderation you can actually audit.

A kids' platform and an adult forum need different lines — and black-box decisions can't be appealed. Aegis scores content across 7 harm categories, applies a per-platform policy, then routes by calibrated confidence: allow, human review, or block. Every decision keeps the offending span, the reasoning and the full routing trace. Live dashboard, running on Groq.

03 / Grounding · refusal

PDFChat

It answers from the page — or not at all.

Agents lie when retrieval gets messy, and a confident wrong answer costs more than no answer. PDFChat extracts and indexes locally, rewrites queries against relevant chunks, and returns answers that cite their pages or refuse clearly. Stateful multi-turn chat and multilingual grounding — ask in another language, still answered only from the document.
